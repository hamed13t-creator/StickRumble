How to play

  • MoveJoystick left / right (keys A / D). Double-tap a direction to dash.
  • Jump & flipJUMP, then JUMP again in the air for a front flip. Flips dodge attacks.
  • Punch / KickFast jab, or a slower kick that reaches further. Kick in the air = flying kick.
  • ComboKeep hitting while the opponent is still reeling: the HITS counter shows your combo. PUNCH, PUNCH, KICK finishes with a Rush that knocks them down.
  • GuardHold GUARD to block. GUARD + KICK = low sweep. GUARD + JUMP = backflip away.
  • ThrowTHROW button (or GUARD + PUNCH) up close. Beats an opponent who keeps blocking.
  • SuperThe SUPER bar fills as you hit and get hit. When it glows, press ★ SUPER for your fighter's special move.
  • CrowdKnock someone into the crowd and the crowd shoves them back: hit them again before they land!
  • ArcadeBeat all 5 other fighters in a row. Clear it with a fighter to earn a gold star, and unlock the GOLD headband.
